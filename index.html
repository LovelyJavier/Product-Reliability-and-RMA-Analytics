Product Reliability and RMA Analytics

1. Project Overview

Analyze RMA data to assess product reliability, customer return patterns, manufacturing quality performance, and identify improvement opportunities through data analytics.

Tools Used: Excel, Power Query, Pivot Tables, Data Visualization

2. Dashboard Overview

3. Business Questions

  • What is the overall Return Material Authorization (RMA) rate?
  • Which manufacturing cohorts have the highest RMA occurrence?
  • When do RMA cases occur frequently by month and week?
  • How does the cumulative RMA rate change as product age increases?
  • Which manufacturing period and components contribute most to RMA?
  • How does cumulative RMA vary across manufacturing cohorts?
  • Which product categories contribute the most failures?
  • What are the most common failure modes reported by customers?

4. Data Preparation

Step Description
Data Sources Combined RMA, Product, Manufacturing, Shipment, and Customer datasets into a single dataset for analysis.
Data Cleaning Used Power Query transformations to remove duplicates, standardize formats, and clean inconsistent values.
Text Extraction Extracted relevant information from Notes field including failure descriptions and product details.
Data Standardization Mapped failure descriptions into standardized failure modes, categories, and issue classifications.
Data Integration Merged manufacturing, shipment, and product information using Power Query relationships.
Output Created a clean analytical dataset used for dashboard development and reliability analysis.

5. Data Validation

Objective: Verify that the total number of RMA records in the dashboard matches the filtered source dataset.

Validation: Compare the total RMA count shown in the Pivot Chart against the filtered records from the source data.

Result: All totals matched, confirming that no records were lost or duplicated during data preparation.

Steps

  1. Apply dashboard filters.
  2. Verify the total number of RMA records shown in the dashboard.
  3. Apply the same filters to the source dataset.

7. Key Findings

Question Dashboard Visual Conclusion
What is the overall Return Material Authorization (RMA) rate in the field? The reported field RMA rate remained at approximately 5–6%, while a clear increase in returns was observed during the later reporting periods. This indicates the need for continued monitoring of newly manufactured product cohorts and recently introduced product changes.
Which manufacturing cohorts have the highest and lowest RMA rates? Manufacturing cohort performance varied significantly across production periods. Several older cohorts showed higher accumulated RMA rates, while more recent cohorts generally demonstrated improved field performance.
When do RMA cases occur most frequently by month and work week? RMA cases were concentrated during specific months and work weeks rather than being evenly distributed over time. The highest return volumes were observed during peak reporting periods, indicating possible shipment, production, or customer-support-related patterns.
How does the cumulative RMA rate change for each quarterly manufacturing cohort? Cumulative RMA rates increased as each cohort aged, with the rate of increase varying by manufacturing period. Some cohorts stabilized after the initial quarters, while others continued to accumulate returns over a longer field-exposure period.
Which manufacturing cohorts and time periods exhibit the highest cumulative RMA rates? The heatmap highlights specific manufacturing cohorts and aging periods with elevated cumulative RMA rates. Darker and warmer cells identify the combinations that require deeper investigation and prioritization.
How do cumulative RMA rates vary across aging intervals for each manufacturing cohort? Failure accumulation was strongest during the early aging intervals for several manufacturing cohorts. Cohorts with continued increases during later intervals may indicate longer-term reliability concerns rather than early-life failures alone.
Which product categories contribute the most failures? A limited number of product categories accounted for a large share of total failures. These categories should receive priority for root-cause analysis, corrective action, and design or process improvement.
What are the most common failure modes reported by customers? The most frequently reported failure modes were concentrated within a small number of recurring customer issues. Addressing these high-frequency modes could reduce a substantial portion of the overall RMA volume.
